# Style profile

The operational guidance lives in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). This note is for maintainers and need not be loaded when writing.

The voice moves from a concrete observation into an explanation or reflection. Its personality comes from the visible movement of thought: a qualification, an everyday analogy, a shift in perspective, or an unresolved tension. Sentence lengths vary. Short observations give longer explanations room to breathe.

Practical writing is comfortable with detailed lists and explicit constraints. Personal writing can be associative and emotionally open without announcing a lesson. Concision means removing empty language, not stripping away reasoning or flattening every piece into a status update.

Everyday conversation is more compressed: answer first, leave shared context implicit, and add a reason or qualification in a short follow-up thought. Fragments and brief acknowledgments are natural. Directness coexists with warmth, enthusiasm, and understated humor. Clean language is an explicit preference for generated text, not a claim that the samples contain no profanity.

These are qualitative inferences from selected Chinese-language essays and direct-chat exchanges from 2025–2026. The chat sample reaches September 9, 2026; it is not exhaustive. Both sides were read for context, but only the author's messages inform the voice. Conversational samples guide everyday replies; reflective, technical, practical, travel, review, and fictional writing guide longer forms. Older fiction sometimes uses heightened imagery. These are defaults, not measured frequencies or universal rules. English guidance transfers those traits; it has not been validated against an English corpus.

Keep only abstract style observations when updating this package. Do not add a source bibliography, article cache, copied examples, identifying details, or recognizable paraphrases. Use invented, unrelated material for checks. This reduces source disclosure in the distributed files; it does not guarantee anonymity or remove material from the conversation or provider logs.
